# Check-out

1. Open the stay from Departures on [the desk](04-desk.md), or from **Bookings**.
2. Take any amount still due. Use the payment on [Arrivals](07-arrivals.md) or the money page in [Reservations](06-reservations.md). Put the slip code on a card or a transfer.
3. Check out.

A deposit still held is applied to the stay at check-out. After that, the bill no longer shows it as money waiting. It has paid the stay.

4. Print the invoice in the house language. See [Letters](15-letters.md).
5. Print the receipt for what was paid.

The invoice total due is what the guest still has to pay. It is not the room price repeated next to the deposit.
